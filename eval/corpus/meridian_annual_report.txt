1. LETTER FROM THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE

Fiscal year 2025 was a year of expansion for Meridian Freight Partners. Unlike
much of the sector, we accelerated new market entry, accepting margin pressure
in exchange for position. Operating margin declined from 9.4% to 6.1% as a
result, which the board considers an acceptable cost of growth.

Group revenue was 2.18 billion euros, up 14.7% on the prior year's 1.90
billion euros. Adjusted EBITDA was 289 million euros. Free cash flow was
negative at minus 44 million euros, reflecting the capital intensity of the
expansion programme.

No dividend is recommended for fiscal 2025. The board intends to review
capital returns once free cash flow is sustainably positive, which management
expects in fiscal 2027.


2. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Meridian reports across four operating segments: Road, Rail, Air Charter, and
Contract Logistics.

2.1 Road

Road contributed 981 million euros of revenue, or 45.0% of the group total.
Segment operating margin was 5.2%. Volumes rose to 4.7 million shipments from
4.1 million. Average revenue per shipment fell from 214 euros to 209 euros,
reflecting competitive pricing in newly entered markets.

The segment operates 2,640 tractor units and 6,100 trailers across nineteen
countries. Average fleet age is 7.1 years, up from 6.8 years, as the expansion
programme took priority over replacement.

2.2 Rail

Rail generated 545 million euros, up 9.1%. Operating margin was 8.4%. The
segment benefits from long-term contracts with three national infrastructure
operators, the earliest of which expires in 2031.

Rail volumes are measured in tonne-kilometres rather than shipments, and
reached 18.4 billion tonne-kilometres.

2.3 Air Charter

Air Charter is the smallest segment at 218 million euros of revenue, up 41.2%
from 154 million euros — the fastest growth in the group. Operating margin was
12.9%, the highest of the four segments.

Growth was driven by time-critical automotive components, which represented
64% of segment revenue. Management cautions that this concentration makes the
segment vulnerable to a single sector's production cycles.

2.4 Contract Logistics

Contract Logistics generated 436 million euros, up 6.8%. Operating margin was
4.9%. The segment operates 41 warehouses totalling 1.2 million square metres.

Warehouse utilisation averaged 84%, below the 90% management considers
efficient. Three underperforming sites in Portugal and Greece are under
review.

4. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND FLEET

Total capital expenditure was 312 million euros, up from 198 million euros.

Capital expenditure by category:

  Warehouse construction   134 million euros
  Vehicle acquisition       98 million euros
  Rail terminal upgrades    47 million euros
  Technology and systems    23 million euros
  Other                     10 million euros

The technology spend of 23 million euros funded a warehouse management system
replacement and the first phase of a telematics rollout. The warehouse system
went live on schedule in August 2025.

Electric vehicles represent 6.2% of the light commercial fleet, up from 3.8%.
Meridian has set a target of 25% by 2030, and unlike several peers has also
committed to a heavy tractor target of 10% by 2032.


5. WORKFORCE

Total headcount at year end was 14,720 employees, up from 12,940. The increase
of 1,780 reflects both organic hiring and the acquisition of a Czech road
haulier in April 2025, which added 640 employees.

Voluntary attrition was 19.6%, up from 18.1%. Driver attrition was 26.4%, and
management acknowledges this as a material operational risk. A pay review is
scheduled for the first quarter of fiscal 2026.

Women represent 24% of total headcount and 15% of senior management roles.

Lost-time injury frequency was 7.2 per million hours worked, up from 6.9.
There was one workplace fatality during the year, at a Czech warehouse site in
September 2025. An external investigation concluded in January 2026 and its
recommendations are being implemented.


6. RISK FACTORS

Fuel price volatility. Approximately 38% of fuel exposure is hedged. A
sustained 10% increase in unhedged diesel prices would reduce operating profit
by an estimated 19 million euros annually.

Customer concentration. The ten largest customers represent 44% of group
revenue. The single largest, an automotive manufacturer served by Air Charter
and Road, represents 11.2% of group revenue.

Integration risk. The Czech acquisition is being integrated onto group systems
during fiscal 2026. Management notes that its two prior acquisitions each took
longer than planned.

Leverage. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA was 3.4 times at year end, up from 2.6
times. The covenant threshold is 4.0 times. Management has stated it will slow
capital expenditure if the ratio approaches 3.8 times.

Regulatory change. Proposed European Union restrictions on driver hours could
reduce effective fleet utilisation. Meridian estimates the impact at between
14 and 22 million euros of annual operating profit.


7. OUTLOOK

For fiscal 2026, management guides to revenue growth of between 10% and 13%,
and operating margin of between 6.0% and 6.8%. Capital expenditure is expected
to fall to approximately 240 million euros as the warehouse programme
completes.

Management expects Air Charter to remain the fastest-growing segment but does
not expect it to exceed 12% of group revenue before fiscal 2028.

The guidance assumes the Czech integration completes without material
disruption and that net debt to adjusted EBITDA does not exceed 3.8 times.
