1. LETTER FROM THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE

Fiscal year 2025 was a year of consolidation for Northwind Logistics Group.
After three years of aggressive expansion, we deliberately slowed new market
entry to focus on margin recovery in the regions we already serve. That
decision cost us roughly four percentage points of revenue growth, and it was
the right one: operating margin recovered from 4.1% to 7.8% over the year.

Our headline result is group revenue of 1.42 billion euros, up 6.2% on the
prior year's 1.34 billion euros. Adjusted EBITDA reached 214 million euros.
Free cash flow turned positive for the first time since 2021, at 61 million
euros.

The board has recommended a dividend of 0.34 euros per share, the first
dividend in the company's history. It will be paid on 14 March 2026 to
shareholders on the register as of 28 February 2026.


2. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Northwind reports across three operating segments: Ground Freight, Cold Chain,
and Last Mile.

2.1 Ground Freight

Ground Freight remains our largest segment, contributing 748 million euros of
revenue, or 52.7% of the group total. Segment operating margin was 8.9%.
Volumes were broadly flat at 3.1 million shipments, but average revenue per
shipment rose from 227 euros to 241 euros following the repricing programme
completed in the second quarter.

The segment operates 1,840 tractor units and 4,210 trailers across fourteen
countries. Average fleet age fell from 6.2 years to 5.4 years after the
capital refresh described in section 4.

2.2 Cold Chain

Cold Chain is our fastest-growing segment, with revenue of 412 million euros,
up 19.4% year on year. It now represents 29.0% of group revenue, compared with
25.7% in the prior year. Operating margin of 11.2% is the highest of the three
segments, reflecting the specialised nature of temperature-controlled freight
and the limited number of competitors able to service it at scale.

Pharmaceutical clients accounted for 58% of Cold Chain revenue, food and
beverage for 34%, and industrial chemicals for the remaining 8%. The segment
added 27 new pharmaceutical accounts during the year, including three of the
ten largest generic manufacturers in Europe.

Cold Chain capacity constraints remain the principal limit on growth. The
segment operated at 91% average utilisation across the year, and above 97% in
the fourth quarter. Two new temperature-controlled facilities, in Rotterdam
and Valencia, are scheduled to open in the second half of 2026.

2.3 Last Mile

Last Mile generated 260 million euros of revenue, down 3.1% from 268 million
euros. This was the only segment to decline. Operating margin was negative at
minus 1.4%, an improvement from minus 5.6% in the prior year but still loss
making.

The decline was concentrated in the consumer electronics vertical, where two
large customers moved volume in-house. Management does not expect Last Mile to
reach breakeven before fiscal 2027.

Northwind has no operations outside Europe and does not currently intend to

4. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND FLEET

Total capital expenditure was 118 million euros, down from 176 million euros
in the prior year. The reduction reflects the completion of the fleet refresh
programme begun in 2023.

Capital expenditure by category:

  Vehicle replacement       61 million euros
  Facility construction     29 million euros
  Technology and systems    19 million euros
  Other                      9 million euros

The technology spend of 19 million euros funded the rollout of the new
transport management system, which replaced four legacy systems inherited
through acquisition. The migration completed in November 2025, eleven weeks
behind the original schedule. The delay was caused by data quality problems in
the legacy French subsidiary's records, which required manual reconciliation
of approximately 240,000 historical shipment records.

Electric vehicles now represent 12.4% of the light commercial fleet, up from
7.1%. The company has committed to 40% by 2030 but has explicitly declined to
set a target for the heavy tractor fleet, citing insufficient charging
infrastructure on long-haul corridors.


5. WORKFORCE

Total headcount at year end was 9,340 employees, compared with 9,180 a year
earlier. The increase of 160 net roles masks significant internal movement:
420 roles were added in Cold Chain operations while 190 were removed from Last
Mile and 70 from central administrative functions.

Voluntary attrition was 14.2%, down from 17.8%. Driver attrition specifically
fell from 24.1% to 18.9% following the pay restructuring agreed with works
councils in March 2025. That restructuring increased driver base pay by an
average of 9.5% and cost 31 million euros annualised.

Women represent 27% of total headcount and 19% of senior management roles. The
company has set a target of 30% of senior management roles by 2028.

Lost-time injury frequency was 4.8 per million hours worked, an improvement
from 6.1. There were no workplace fatalities during the year.


6. RISK FACTORS

Management considers the following the principal risks to the business.

Fuel price volatility. Approximately 62% of fuel exposure is hedged on a
rolling twelve-month basis. A sustained 10% increase in unhedged diesel prices
would reduce operating profit by an estimated 11 million euros annually.

Customer concentration. The ten largest customers represent 31% of group
revenue. The single largest customer, a pharmaceutical distributor served by
Cold Chain, represents 6.8% of group revenue. The loss of that customer would
be material.

Regulatory change. Proposed European Union restrictions on driver hours could
reduce effective fleet utilisation. Management estimates the impact at between
8 and 14 million euros of annual operating profit, depending on the final form
of the legislation.

Cyber security. The company experienced two attempted ransomware intrusions
during the year, both contained without data loss or operational disruption.
Security spending increased to 4.2 million euros from 2.6 million euros.

Integration risk. Three acquisitions completed between 2022 and 2024 are not
yet fully integrated onto common systems. Management expects integration to
complete during fiscal 2027.


7. OUTLOOK

For fiscal 2026, management guides to revenue growth of between 5% and 8%, and
operating margin of between 8.0% and 8.5%. Capital expenditure is expected to
be approximately 130 million euros, weighted toward the two new Cold Chain
facilities.

Management expects Cold Chain to exceed 30% of group revenue during fiscal
2026 and to become the largest contributor to group operating profit, despite
Ground Freight remaining larger by revenue.

The guidance assumes no material deterioration in European industrial output
and no adverse final outcome from the driver-hours legislation described in
section 6.
